Brand Culture & Service Philosophy

Welcome to your third home.

Sleepless Cafe is designed to be the ultimate local "Tambayan"—a chill, high-vibes space where friends hang out, reset, work, and connect. We serve top-tier coffee, non-coffee craft blends, and sparkling fruit sodas without snobbery.

Concept: Cool, Unpretentious & Chill
Team Structure: 2-Person Synchronized Crew
Menu: Espresso, Non-Coffee & Sparklers (No Food)

The 3-Second Rule

Greet every customer within 3 seconds of stepping through the door. A quick, genuine "Hey! Welcome to Sleepless!" instantly establishes our warm, friendly vibe.

Makes guests feel noticed immediately

Ambience & Soundscape

Keep background music tailored to the hour (chill lofi / acoustic pop in morning, warm upbeat indie in afternoon). Temperature should remain crisp (22°C–24°C).

Controlled by FOH Specialist

Graceful Remakes

If a guest is unhappy with a drink taste, never argue. Listen intently, apologize warmly, and remake the drink on the house right away or suggest a preferred alternative.

Priority: Customer retention > drink cost

The 2-Person Synchronized Flow

Step 1
Greeting & POS

FOH greets guest, inputs order in POS, collects cash/e-payment, and calls order out clearly to BOH.

Step 2
Dual Execution

BOH pulls espresso shots / steams milk. FOH builds sparklers/soda drinks or manages guest seating.

Step 3
Staging & QC

BOH passes finished espresso items to staging counter. FOH double-checks order completeness and wipes cup drips.

Step 4
Warm Handoff

FOH calls guest's name, hands over drinks with straws/napkins, and invites them to enjoy their stay.

Front of House (FOH) Specialist

Primary Focus: Customer Experience, POS, Sparklers, Lounge Vibes

Guest Facing

Key Operational Responsibilities

  • POS & Cashier: Greet customers instantly, take orders accurately, collect payments (cash/E-wallets).
  • Sparkler & Soda Station: Craft all fruit syrup and sparkling soda drinks with perfect layering.
  • Lounge Maintenance: Clear tables promptly after guests leave; keep seating area spotlessly clean.
  • Vibe Control: Manage background music playlists and maintain comfortable aircon cooling.

Order Call-Out Standards

"Order for [Name]: 1 Iced Spanish Latte, 1 Passionfruit Sparkler!"

Always state guest name, drink size, milk modifications (e.g. Oat milk), and temperature clearly so BOH can queue shots immediately.

Back of House (BOH) / Bar Specialist

Primary Focus: Espresso Dial-in, Milk Texture, Speed on Bar, QC

Production Bar

Key Operational Responsibilities

  • Espresso Calibration: Dial in grinder every morning (18 g in → 36 g out in 27–30 s).
  • Beverage Production: Pull espresso shots, steam silky microfoam milk, craft Matcha & Chocolate blends.
  • Bar Cleanliness: Purge and wipe steam wand before & after every pitcher. Keep drip tray clean.
  • Inventory Watch: Monitor ice bin level, espresso bean hopper level, and milk fridge stock.

Equipment Maintenance & Troubleshooting

Issue: Clogged Fruit Syrup Pump

Pump stem gets sticky, stuck down, or dispenses uneven syrup volume.

SOP Fix Action:

Remove pump mechanism. Submerge in warm water (60°C) for 5 mins. Pump warm water through mechanism until clear, dry, and reattach.

Issue: Espresso Group Head Pressure Drop

Extraction gauge drops below standard 9-bar pressure during shot pull.

SOP Fix Action:

Inspect water inlet hose line for kinks. Brush group head rubber gasket to clear compressed grounds. If unresolved, contact shop owner.

Cash Handling & Till Policy

Opening Cash Float

Register float starts at exactly ₱3,000.00 in small denominations (₱20, ₱50, ₱100 bills and coins). Verify and log every morning before doors open.

Safe Drop Rule

Whenever total cash in drawer exceeds ₱10,000.00, FOH must conduct a mid-day cash drop into the secure drop safe to minimize counter risk.

Discrepancy SOP Protocol:
  • Minor Variance (≤ ±₱100): Record in shift log with note (e.g. *"Incorrect change during lunch rush"*).
  • Major Variance (> ±₱100): Re-count cash together (FOH & BOH), cross-check digital e-wallet sales, and notify manager immediately.
